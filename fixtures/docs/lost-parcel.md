# Lost parcel after a delivery scan

When tracking shows delivered and the customer cannot find the parcel, agents:

1. Confirm the tracking number and the delivery scan time.
2. Ask the customer to check the doorstep, mail room, and neighbors.
3. Open a critical shipping ticket if the parcel is still missing after that check.

A replacement ships only after the carrier trace has been open for one business day, unless the order was marked fragile.
