# Refund window

Northline Goods accepts a refund request for unused items within 14 calendar days of delivery.

Agents should:

1. Confirm the order date and that the item was not marked final sale.
2. Open a billing ticket if the refund has to go to a different card than the one charged.
3. Tell the customer the refund window is 14 calendar days, not 14 business days.

Store credit is offered only after the customer asks for it. Do not promise a same-day card posting.
